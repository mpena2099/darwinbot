DarwinBot

DarwinBot evolves buildable robots: MAP-Elites searches over bodies made of real parts (Feetech STS3215 servos, printed links, ESP32 and LiPo), and CMA-ES tunes a rhythmic gait with servo-load feedback for each body in MuJoCo. Pick a robot on the map to watch its gait. Source on GitHub.
